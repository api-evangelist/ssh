Privileged access has always been one of the most critical—and most exploited—attack vectors in the enterprise. As the Gartner® Buyers’ Guide for Privileged Access Management highlights, PAM implementations can be complex and costly and require careful resource planning and negotiation with vendors. Traditional approaches to PAM often fall short because they fail to address a growing and fundamental security objective : eliminating standing privileges wherever possible.
