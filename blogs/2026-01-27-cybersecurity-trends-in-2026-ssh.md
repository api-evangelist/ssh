Cybersecurity in 2026 looks very different from just a few years ago. The challenge today isn’t only about protecting systems, but about securing how organizations access critical data and systems, communicate, operate, and make decisions. Across identity security secure collaboration, and operational technology (OT), one message is consistent: organizations want more control, flexibility, and greater resilience while maintaining the utmost security
