Data sovereignty is no longer a theoretical concept reserved for policymakers. It is quickly becoming a defining factor in how European organizations choose, evaluate, and manage their digital tools - especially when it comes to communication. Across Europe, a shift is taking place. Governments and enterprises alike are taking a closer look at where their data resides, who can access it, and under which legal framework it ultimately falls.
