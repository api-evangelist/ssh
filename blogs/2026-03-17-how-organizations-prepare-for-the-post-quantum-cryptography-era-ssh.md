As quantum computing advances, organizations face a fundamental shift in how they must protect sensitive data. Encryption systems that have secured the internet and enterprise networks for decades will eventually become vulnerable to cryptographically relevant quantum computers (CRQC). This looming change is accelerating the adoption of post-quantum cryptography (PQC) and forcing organizations to rethink how encryption is deployed across their networks. The challenge is technical and architectural. Enterprises must transition toward quantum-safe encryption while maintaining compatibility with existing systems and infrastructure.
