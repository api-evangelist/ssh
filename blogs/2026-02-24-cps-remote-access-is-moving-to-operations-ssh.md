Remote access has become a permanent operational reality for cyber-physical systems (CPS), what was once an occasional maintenance tool is now a foundational capability for operating, maintaining, and supporting industrial and critical infrastructure environments. At the same time, remote access is rapidly becoming one of the most exploited attack vectors into CPS environments. This tension — between operational necessity and rising risk — is driving a fundamental shift in how organizations approach remote access.
