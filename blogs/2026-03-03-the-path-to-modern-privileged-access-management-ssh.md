Privileged Access Management has changed dramatically over the past few years. What was once focused almost entirely on vaulting passwords and brokering administrative sessions has expanded into something much broader. Modern organizations now expect identity-driven access, integrated network controls, and cryptographic foundations that can defend against future threats. These changes in expectations have been punctuated recently by certain market consolidations.
