Matrix.org continues to strengthen its position as the leading open-source secure communication platform. As part of our ongoing collaboration with Element, SSH’s SalaX Secure Messaging team attended the Matrix Conference 2025 in Berlin to explore how new advancements in the Matrix ecosystem are shaping the future of secure, decentralized communication. This year’s event made one thing clear: Matrix is entering a new phase of maturity, focused on stability, scalability, and usability.
