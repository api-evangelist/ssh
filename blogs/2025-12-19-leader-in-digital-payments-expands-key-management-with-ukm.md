Helsinki, Finland - December 19, 2024 - A global leader in digital payments has further improved their SSH key governance capabilities by expanding their Universal SSH Key Manager (UKM) deal with SSH Communications Security (SSH). The expansion is worth 170k USD, with the total value of the deal amounting to around 500k USD.
