DevOps is often the engine that drives rapid market growth and innovation in an enterprise. Its aim is to shorten the software development life cycle and deliver high-quality applications and services more rapidly. It bridges the gap between software development (Dev) and IT operations (Ops) , promoting collaboration, automation, and continuous improvement.
